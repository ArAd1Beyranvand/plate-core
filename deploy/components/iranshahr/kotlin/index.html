Iranshahr license plates in Kotlin

The library draws 93 plate formats for Iranshahr, among them Civilian (EU size), Civilian (US size), Bicycle and Diplomatic corps. The region or district on a plate is looked up from its code and shown beside the number. A typical registration reads 123456. This page runs the Kotlin port: type a registration and watch the plate take shape and get checked as you go.
