Democratic Republic Of The Congo license plates in Python

The library draws 6 plate formats for Democratic Republic Of The Congo, among them Private car, International organisation, FARDC and National Police. The region or district on a plate is looked up from its code and shown beside the number. A typical registration reads 1234AB10. This page runs the Python port on Pyodide: type a registration and watch the plate take shape and get checked as you go.
