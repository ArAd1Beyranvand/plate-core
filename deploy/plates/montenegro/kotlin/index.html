Montenegro license plates in Kotlin

The library draws 5 plate formats for Montenegro, among them Regular, Temporary, Diplomat and Press agency. The region or district on a plate is looked up from its code and shown beside the number. A typical registration reads PGQZ581. This page runs the Kotlin port: type a registration and watch the plate take shape and get checked as you go.
